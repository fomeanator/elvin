The name bubble is intended to stretch horizontally and overlap the dialogue
frame's upper edge. Its lower edge is straight; the speaker name is rendered by
the engine as separate text. Suggested text inset: left 22, right 18, top 13,
bottom 8 px at @1x.

The four clipped corners, cyan plates and all thickness changes are contained
inside the fixed corner zones. The center and the long middle sections of every
edge are homogeneous, so the sprite may be safely stretched in both directions.

RGBA PNG, 72 dpi, no embedded ICC profile. No text or external shadow.
